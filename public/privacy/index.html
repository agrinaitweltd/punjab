Privacy Policy

Last updated: 23 July 2026

Punjab Exotic Foods Ltd ("we", "us", "our") operates the customer and trade portal at punjabexoticfoods.com. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our customers and the people who use our website. This policy explains what personal information we collect, why we collect it, and how we look after it, in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR) and the Data Protection Act 2018.

What we collect

  • Account details — company name, contact name, email address, phone number, delivery address and customer number, so we can run your trade account.
  • Order and payment records — the orders you place, invoices, statements and payments, so we can supply goods and manage credit terms.
  • Sign-in information — your login credentials (stored securely) and, if you sign in with Google, the name and email address Google shares with us.
  • Technical data — basic device and usage information needed to keep the portal working and secure.

Why we use it

  • To provide the wholesale ordering service and fulfil your orders (performance of a contract).
  • To invoice you, take payments, and manage credit accounts (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To send service emails — order confirmations, payment receipts, account and payment reminders (contract and legitimate interests).
  • To keep the portal secure and prevent misuse (legitimate interests and legal obligations).

We do not sell your personal information, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

Who we share it with

We use a small number of trusted service providers to run the portal: secure database hosting, email delivery, postcode/address lookup, and card payment processing (Stripe — we never see or store your card details). Each provider only receives what it needs to do its job.

How long we keep it

We keep account, order and payment records for as long as you hold an account with us and afterwards for as long as UK tax and accounting law requires (normally six years). We delete or anonymise information we no longer need.

Your rights

You have the right to access the personal information we hold about you, to correct it, to ask us to delete it, to restrict or object to how we use it, and to data portability. To exercise any of these rights, email info@punjabexoticfoods.com. If you're unhappy with how we've handled your information, you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Cookies

The portal uses strictly necessary cookies and local storage to keep you signed in and remember your preferences. You can manage optional cookies from the cookie banner shown when you first visit the site.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available at this address, with the date of the last update shown at the top.
